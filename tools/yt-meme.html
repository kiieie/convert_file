💡 YouTube Clip Meme Maker

Paste any YouTube link, pick the exact start and end seconds of the moment you want, and drop meme-style text bars above and below the clip. The result is a shareable clip link (and embed code) that plays only that segment with your caption baked into the page — no editing software required.

Because the YouTube player is a remote embed, its video frames can't be read pixel-by-pixel by the browser, so a real animated GIF can't be produced directly from the stream. If you need an actual GIF file, upload a local copy of the video (your own recording or source file) covering the same segment, and the tool renders the GIF — complete with your meme text burned into the frames — entirely on your device.

Every step, from segment selection to GIF rendering, runs 100% client-side. No files are uploaded to a server, so your clips and captions stay private.
